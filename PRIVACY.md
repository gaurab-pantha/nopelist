# Nopelist privacy policy

last updated: 2 November 2026

short version: Nopelist doesn't collect, sell or share anything about you.

## what it saves

only on your own computer (in `chrome.storage.local`):
- your list of blocked sites
- your on/off, pause, schedule and lock settings
- your block page message
- light or dark mode
- how many times each site stopped you, in total and per day (kept for 60 days)

the light/dark choice is also saved in the extension's 'localStorage` so pages open in the right color. none of this ever leaves your device.

## what it doesn't do

- doesn't read the content of pages
- doesn't track your browsing history
- no analytics, no trackers
- doesn't talk to any server. it makes no network requests of its own
- the font is inside the extension, nothing is loaded from the internet

## why it needs access to all sites

chrome only lets an extension redirect pages if it has access to sites. that's how the block page works. Nopelist only redirects pages that are on your list, and only while blocking is on.

when a page gets blocked, the address you were trying to open is passed to the block page inside your browser (you can see it in the block page's address). that's so the "continue" button can take you back there. Nopelist doesn't save it or send it anywhere. your browser might keep it in tab history like any other page.

## links

the "by gaurab", portfolio, GitHub and SSIRC links only open if you click them. they go to other websites that have their own privacy policies.

## your control

you can remove sites, reset the counters or clear everything in the settings page. removing the extension deletes everything it saved.

## changes

if this changes, the date at the top changes too.
