good to know

  • a site always includes its subdomains. blocking google.com also blocks mail.google.com.
  • youtube.com/shorts blocks just that part of the site.
  • lock me in (in the popup) can't be undone until the timer ends. use it before exams.
  • shortcut: Alt+Shift+B turns blocking on or off. change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • right click any page or link, then "Block this site".
  • sites that change pages without reloading (like YouTube) show the block page on the next reload or new tab.
  • for incognito: chrome://extensions, Details on Site Blocker, then "Allow in Incognito".
  • everything stays on this computer. nothing is sent anywhere.
